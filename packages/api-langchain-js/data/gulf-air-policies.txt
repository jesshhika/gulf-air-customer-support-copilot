Gulf Air Customer Support Policy

1. Disruption policy: passengers affected by delays of more than 2 hours may request rebooking on the next available flight, subject to availability and fare conditions.

2. Cancelled flights are eligible for:
   - Full refund when the cancellation is within Gulf Air control and no travel occurred
   - Free rebooking to the next available service where feasible
   - Travel voucher when alternative travel is unavailable or when a fare-rule exception applies

3. Meal vouchers are provided for delays exceeding 3 hours when the disruption is within Gulf Air control and passenger duty time is extended.

4. Hotel accommodation may be offered for overnight disruptions when rebooking is not possible on the same day and when the disruption is a qualifying event.

5. Customer service may assist with alternative routing, protected connections, and recovery options during operational disruption.

6. Refund eligibility must be determined by the booked fare, ticket conditions, and whether the disruption was caused by airline operations or passenger-initiated changes.

7. Baggage service requests must be handled with care. Lost, delayed, or damaged baggage claims must be documented, traced, and resolved using the baggage claim workflow.

8. Special assistance policies apply to unaccompanied minors, wheelchair requests, medical needs, PRMs, and families travelling with children. Requests should be prioritised and escalated when necessary.

9. FalconFlyer loyalty members receive tier-aware assistance, including mileage guidance, lounge access, upgrades, and offer eligibility checks. Promotions and offers must be clearly explained with any conditions attached.

10. Sensitive cases such as medical incidents, bereavement, legal threats, harassment, or formal complaints must be escalated to a human agent with a full case summary and customer-safe handoff.

11. All customer-facing responses must be bilingual where appropriate, with Arabic and English support and professional tone. Do not invent compensation, make promises outside policy, or confirm claims without verified record access.

12. If vital information is missing, the agent must ask only the minimum required follow-up questions before finalising recommendations.
