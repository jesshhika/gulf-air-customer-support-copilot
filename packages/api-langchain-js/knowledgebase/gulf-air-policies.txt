Gulf Air Disruption Policy

1. Passengers affected by delays greater than 2 hours may request rebooking on the next available flight.

2. Cancelled flights are eligible for:
   - Full refund
   - Free rebooking
   - Travel voucher

3. Meals are provided for delays exceeding 3 hours.

4. Hotel accommodation may be offered for overnight disruptions.

5. Customer service can assist with alternative routing options.
